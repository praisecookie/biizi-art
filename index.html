The Artist & Her Story

Born in Akwa Ibom State and based in the vibrant cultural heart of Lagos, Annette Udoh—known professionally as biizi art—is a self-taught multidisciplinary artist. Taught by God and inspired early on by watching her older sister draw, Annette bridges classical technique with contemporary expression.

Working across oils, acrylics on canvas, watercolors, and fashion illustrations, her work celebrates nature, everyday lifestyle, and the profound depth of female human faces. Painting remains her meditative anchor—bringing emotional balance, capturing raw energy, and leaving indelible memories glued to the minds of her viewers.
