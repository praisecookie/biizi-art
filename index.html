Her story

It started with a pencil and an older sister who could draw.

Annette Udoh grew up in Akwa Ibom State and now paints from Lagos. As a child she sat and watched her sister sketch, then picked up a pencil herself. Nobody taught her what came next. She says she was "taught by God".

Faces were her first subject and they still are. She paints women because a face can point a viewer in a dozen directions at once. Pen and pencil remain her starting point, even as she moves between oil, acrylic, watercolour and digital work.

Her best-known moment so far was an invitation to the Audacity Project, where she painted a well-known figure live in front of a seated audience.
